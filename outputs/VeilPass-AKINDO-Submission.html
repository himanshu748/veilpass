Full project description

Age gates and eligibility checks routinely collect more identity data than a decision requires. A verifier may need only a yes-or-no answer, yet users are often asked to expose a full birth date or identity document. That creates avoidable breach risk, retention obligations and user distrust.

VeilPass demonstrates a narrower disclosure model. A user supplies a birth year locally, selects a test issuer state and evaluates the policy “Age is 18 or older?” Compiler-generated Compact code returns a minimal receipt containing the result, policy identifier, issuer-verification state and proof identifier. The birth year, credential payload and nonce remain private.

The conversion-focused landing experience makes this boundary visible before the working proof lab, then reinforces it through a sealed aperture, fail-closed current-session verifier and public-only activity log. Under the interface, a Compact contract receives a PrivateCredential through a private witness and writes only a ProofReceipt to its in-memory Compact ledger. The contract compiles with Compact 0.31.1. Ten automated tests cover age boundaries, private-field leakage, forged links, malformed fields, cross-session receipts and policy binding.

The browser imports the compiler-generated bindings, executes createEligibilityProof through the Compact runtime and authenticates receipts only when every disclosed field matches the trusted same-session ledger entry. This is real Compact execution, but not yet zero-knowledge proof generation or a Preprod transaction. The next milestone is to connect Lace, a proof server, a deployed Preprod contract and a real credential issuer.

Selective disclosure can extend beyond age checks to membership, licensing, compliance and healthcare eligibility. VeilPass shows how a business can learn the policy answer it needs without becoming a database of sensitive identity facts.

Judge demo path

  2. Inspect the sealed proof state and the minimal public receipt.
  3. Copy the local receipt link and authenticate it on Verify without refreshing.
  4. Open Activity and confirm that private credential fields are absent.
  5. Modify one receipt field and confirm that verification fails closed.
  6. Review contract/src/veilpass.compact and run all ten tests.

Known limitations

  • The browser executes generated Compact code locally, without zero-knowledge proof generation
  • No production credential issuer is connected
  • No Lace wallet or Preprod deployment is included yet
  • The verifier authenticates only against the current in-memory receipt registry
  • Cross-session links intentionally fail closed until Midnight ledger verification is connected
  • The calendar-year policy needs a full-date replacement for production
